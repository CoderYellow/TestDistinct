# How to deliver your introduction

| Interview prompt | Recommended response |
| --- | --- |
| Tell me about yourself. | Three-minute introduction |
| Could you briefly introduce yourself? | Shorten to one or two minutes |
| Walk me through your CV. | Five-minute introduction |
| Tell me about your Java experience. | Focus on HSBC and Neusoft |
| What have you been doing recently? | Focus on DBS |

## Useful transitions

**DBS to HSBC:**
> Before joining NCS, I worked on a foreign exchange order management system for HSBC, which was more focused on Java backend development.

**Why the recent work is still backend development:**
> Although my recent role involves data platforms and operational automation, I've remained hands-on with backend development.

**Technical strength:**
> My main strength is that I understand not only how to build backend applications, but also how to operate and troubleshoot them in production.

## Rehearsal
- Use a natural conversational pace; time yourself instead of trusting a word-count estimate.
- Prioritise the three-minute version for the first interview.
- Be ready to stop at a natural transition if the interviewer interrupts.
- Be specific about personal contribution; never invent TPS figures, measured improvements or system guarantees.
- Keep confidential banking project information out of public notes.
